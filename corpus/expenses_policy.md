# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General Guidelines
All business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business.
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense.

## 2. Travel Expenses
Flights must be booked in economy class. Upgrades are at the employee's personal expense.
Hotel accommodations should not exceed $250 per night in major cities, or $150 per night elsewhere.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day (per diem).

## 3. Client Entertainment
Client dinners and entertainment require pre-approval from a Vice President if the total cost exceeds $500.

## 4. Receipts
Itemized receipts are required for all expenses exceeding $25. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient.
