# Benefits Policy

## 1. Health Insurance
The company offers Gold, Silver, and Bronze health insurance plans. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the date of hire.

## 2. Retirement (401k)
The company matches 100% of employee 401k contributions up to 4% of the employee's base salary. Matching begins after 6 months of employment.
Vesting is immediate.

## 3. Dental and Vision
Optional dental and vision coverage is available. The company subsidizes 50% of the premium for these plans.

## 4. Wellness Stipend
Employees receive a $50 monthly stipend for wellness-related expenses, such as gym memberships or mental health apps. This is reimbursed through the standard expense system.
