# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility
Employees are eligible for full-time remote work if their role does not require physical presence in the office. Role eligibility is determined by the department head.
Employees must maintain a satisfactory performance rating to remain eligible for remote work.

## 2. Work Location
Remote employees must primarily work from their approved home address. Working from a different state or country for more than 30 consecutive days requires HR and tax compliance approval.

## 3. Equipment and Expenses
The company provides a standard laptop and a one-time $500 stipend for home office setup.
Monthly internet and phone bills are not reimbursed unless explicitly required for the role and pre-approved by the manager.

## 4. Data Security
Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems over public Wi-Fi.
All devices used for work must have company-approved mobile device management (MDM) software installed.
