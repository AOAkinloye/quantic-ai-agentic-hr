# Code of Conduct

## 1. Respectful Workplace
We are committed to providing a work environment free from discrimination and harassment. All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect.

## 2. Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where their personal interests conflict with the interests of the company. Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately.

## 3. Confidentiality
Employees must protect the company's confidential information, including trade secrets, financial data, and client information. This obligation continues even after employment terminates.

## 4. Reporting Violations
Employees are encouraged to report any suspected violations of this Code of Conduct to their manager, HR, or through the anonymous whistleblower hotline. Retaliation against anyone who makes a good faith report is strictly prohibited.
