# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## 1. Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 120 hours (15 days) of PTO per year during their first 3 years of service.
After 3 years, employees accrue 160 hours (20 days) per year.
Sick leave is provided separately at 56 hours (7 days) per year, available upfront on January 1st.

## 2. Requesting PTO
All PTO requests must be submitted through the HR system.
Requests for 1-2 days off must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance.
Requests for 3 or more days off must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
Manager approval is required for all PTO requests.

## 3. Rollover
Employees can roll over up to 40 hours of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Any PTO exceeding 40 hours will be forfeited on December 31st.

## 4. Sick Leave
Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for a sick family member. Unused sick leave does not roll over.
